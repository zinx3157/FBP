Mobile PWA

Install LabelOnZeWay.

The app installs from your browser—no App Store or Play Store package is required. After its first successful load, the core interface is cached for offline use.

Open LabelOnZeWay
A

Android · Chrome

  1. Open LabelOnZeWay in Chrome.
  2. Open the browser menu.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm Install.
i

iPhone / iPad · Safari

  1. Open LabelOnZeWay in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.
Data is device-specific. Installing on a second phone or using another browser does not automatically copy profiles or parcels. Use the app's Backup and Restore controls to transfer or protect your records. See the printing guide for POS80C and iOS details.
